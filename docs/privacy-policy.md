# Gym Bros Bro Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 14, 2026

Gym Bros Bro is a workout tracker for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. Short version: **it stays on your phone.** The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

## What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your phone:

- Workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weights, times and distances.
- Workout templates, your training schedule, and custom exercises you create.
- Bodyweight entries you choose to enter.
- Your player name, avatar, trophies and app settings.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives it. The app has no servers, and it does not request the Android internet permission, so it has no way to send your information anywhere.

## Backups

- **Android Auto Backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in the backup it saves to your Google account, as it does for most apps. Google performs and encrypts that backup, not the app, and it is covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.
- **Export files.** The Export button in Settings saves a backup file to a place you pick, such as your phone's Downloads folder or Google Drive. The app only writes the file where you tell it to. What happens to the file after that is up to you.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for optional workout reminders, which are off unless you turn them on.
- **Run at startup:** so a reminder you set still arrives after your phone restarts.
- **Vibration:** for optional haptic feedback.

## Sharing

The app does not sell or share your information with anyone, because the developer never has it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Export files you saved, and any Google backup, are under your control in those places.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
